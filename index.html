01 · The problem

Convincing is not the same as correct.

AI responses can look polished while still containing:

Factual errors, missing requirements, unsupported claims, contradictions, hallucinations, overconfident language, or unsafe recommendations.

The solution

Turn repetitive manual review into a structured evaluation workflow with explicit criteria, scoring, error detection, and actionable improvement feedback.

03 · Evaluation framework

Seven dimensions. One explainable score.

The weights below are the design choices of this project, not an industry-wide standard. They prioritize factual correctness while preserving usefulness and safety.

CriterionWeightWhat is checked?
Accuracy30%Factual correctness, unsupported certainty, contradictions.
Relevance15%Directly addresses the request without distracting detours.
Completeness15%Covers important requirements, constraints and requested outputs.
Reasoning15%Conclusions follow from coherent logic, calculations or evidence.
Instruction Following10%Respects the requested format, tone and constraints.
Clarity & Style10%Readable, structured and unambiguous communication.
Safety5%Avoids harmful, deceptive or inappropriate guidance.
04 · Live case study

A simple factual error — detected immediately.

User prompt

What is the capital of Australia?

AI response

“Sydney is the capital of Australia.”

FAIL · 42/100

Major issue: factual accuracy failure.

The answer gives an incorrect capital. The correct answer is Canberra. Because the core request is factual, this error materially affects the usefulness of the response.

Action: correct the factual claim and verify critical facts before reuse.
05 · Key capabilities

Built for practical AI quality review.

🎯 Accuracy EvaluationFind factual errors and unsupported certainty.
🔎 Hallucination RiskFlag claims that need verification instead of treating fluency as evidence.
📋 Instruction FollowingCheck whether the response actually follows the user's constraints.
📊 Structured ScoringTurn qualitative review into a consistent /100 score.
⚖️ Human-in-the-loopKeep human verification where accuracy or risk matters.
💡 Actionable FeedbackPrioritize the improvements that matter most instead of only reporting a score.
06 · Project architecture

Simple by design.

USER PROMPT + AI RESPONSE ↓ GEM INSTRUCTIONS ↓ QUALITY EVALUATION RUBRIC ↓ SCORE + FINDINGS + VERDICT ↓ IMPROVEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS ↓ HUMAN REVIEW / REUSE
Positioning: This is a practical Gemini-based evaluation assistant, not a replacement for large-scale automated evaluation frameworks. It is intentionally optimized for fast, explainable human review.
